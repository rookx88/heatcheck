Two Roads in the Fourth
Don't be fooled by the matching 2-1 records over the last three games. These are two teams on fundamentally different trajectories. The Hornets generate blowouts but crumble in tight spots. The Magic live in the margins, weathering storms and finding ways to win when it matters most. Tonight's game isn't about who is better, but whose style will dictate the final, critical minutes.
Thesis
The Hornets and Magic enter with identical 5-5 records over their last ten, but their paths diverge sharply when the pressure mounts. Charlotte has amassed an impressive +10.4 point margin but remains winless in close contests, posting a telling "CHA 0-3" record in games decided by six points or less. Orlando, conversely, thrives in the crucible of late-game execution, setting the stage for a test of Charlotte's composure against the Magic's proven grit.

Pressure Points
- The final 5 minutes if the margin is under 6 points—Orlando's territory.
- Paolo Banchero's performance, who is shouldering an increased offensive load with a USG10 of 24.2.
- Whether Charlotte can build a double-digit lead before the fourth quarter, effectively taking the clutch scenario off the table.
- Orlando's start. Another slow opening, as mentioned by Coach Mosley, could put them in a hole too deep to climb out of, even for them.
- The shot-making of LaMelo Ball and Brandon Miller down the stretch against a locked-in Magic defense.
What This Means
This game serves as a crucial litmus test for both squads. For the Hornets, it's an opportunity to prove they can mature beyond being a team that only wins with overwhelming force, and learn to secure a victory through poise. For the Magic, it's a chance to reinforce their identity as a tough, disciplined playoff team that thrives on pressure, demonstrating that their grit is a reliable weapon, not a recent fluke.
