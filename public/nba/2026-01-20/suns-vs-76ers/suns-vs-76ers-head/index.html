Pressure Points
- Devin Booker attacking a Sixers defense without Embiid's rim protection.
- Tyrese Maxey's shot selection down the stretch as the sole primary creator.
- The offensive contributions of Kelly Oubre Jr. and other Sixers role players who must step into a vacuum.
- Phoenix's ability to control the tempo against a team that may try to create chaos.
- Can Philadelphia survive the minutes when Maxey is on the bench?
What This Means
This game is a stress test for both franchises. For the 76ers, it's a stark preview of their identity without their MVP, forcing them to define who they are around Tyrese Maxey. A competitive loss shows resilience; a blowout raises serious questions about their ceiling. For the Suns, this is a referendum on their killer instinct. Contenders don't just beat wounded teams, they dominate them. This is a chance for Phoenix to prove they have the maturity to handle business on the road and solidify their place in the brutal Western Conference.
