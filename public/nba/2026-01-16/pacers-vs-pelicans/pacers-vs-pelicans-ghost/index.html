The Ghost of Haliburton

// The Pacers are desperately trying to prove they are more than a one-man team, fueled by the ghost of their fallen superstar.

> MATCHUP: NBA | Indiana Pacers vs New Orleans Pelicans | DATE: 2026-01-16

The Ghost in the Machine
The heart of the Indiana Pacers was ripped out on the court in Game 7 of the NBA Finals. Tonight, we find out if the ghost is enough to win.
Ignition

This isn't just another mid-January NBA game. This is a stress test of a team's soul. The Indiana Pacers are without their franchise cornerstone, Tyrese Haliburton, for the entire season. They are also missing key offensive weapon Bennedict Mathurin and frontcourt players Obi Toppin and Isaiah Jackson. By all logical metrics, their season should be over. Yet, they enter this game on a three-game winning streak, a defiant roar in the face of catastrophic injury. Across the court stands a New Orleans Pelicans team in freefall, having lost nine of their last ten games. For them, this game is an indictment: if you can't beat a team this wounded, what are you?
Tension Build

The pressure in this matchup is suffocating. For the Pacers, every win without Haliburton is a validation of their culture and a testament to their professional pride. A loss, especially at home to a struggling team, threatens to pop the bubble of their resilience and force them to confront the grim reality of their situation. For the Pelicans, the stakes are even higher. A loss tonight would be a catastrophic signal to the locker room, the front office, and the fanbase that the team's issues run deeper than talent. It's a potential point of no return for a season already spiraling out of control.
Receipts

* The Defining Blow: On June 22, 2025, the Pacers confirmed their worst fears: Tyrese Haliburton, the engine of their offense and the face of their franchise, would miss the entire 2025-26 season with a torn Achilles.
* A Roster Decimated: The hits kept coming. The Pacers will take the floor tonight without not only Haliburton, but also Bennedict Mathurin (thumb), Obi Toppin (hamstring), and Isaiah Jackson (concussion).
* Pelicans in Collapse: The Pelicans' season is cratering. They have just one victory in their last ten outings, a stretch of basketball that has put coach Willie Green squarely on the hot seat.
* Coach Under Fire: Speaking on the team's disastrous start earlier this season, Coach Green admitted the collective frustration: "We're all frustrated, want to be better. We're all a part of making sure that we do what we can to be better."
Human Moment

Pascal Siakam was brought to Indiana to be the veteran co-star, the championship-tested running mate for a brilliant young point guard. He now finds himself the sole pillar of a team that has been stripped of its identity. Every huddle, every possession, every late-game situation now falls on his shoulders. He is no longer the second option; he is the only option. He must now lead not just with his scoring and defense, but with his will, trying to convince a roster of role players that the ghost of the player they lost is a presence that can elevate them, not an absence that will doom them.
Edge Transition

The betting markets are trying to solve an impossible emotional equation. The Indiana Pacers are favorites at home, with a Moneyline price of 1.65, a line that seems to honor their recent surge of resilience. But it also reflects the utter collapse of the New Orleans Pelicans. This game isn't about talent vs. talent; it's about desperation vs. defiance. The lines are pricing in the Pacers' fighting spirit against the Pelicans' apparent lack of it. For bettors, the challenge is to determine whether Indiana's emotional momentum is sustainable or if the sheer weight of their injuries will finally cause them to break against a Pelicans team that, on paper, should still have an advantage.
