This isn't just about talent; it's a test of process and nerve. The central conflict boils down to how each team handles the immense pressure of the final minutes. One team has a proven identity built on composure, while the other is trying to forge one from the wreckage of close losses.
The Decisive Pressure Point
The data paints a stark picture of this execution gap. In high-leverage situations, the behavioral patterns of these two teams diverge completely.

For the Nuggets, a perfect 5-0 record reflects a deeply ingrained calm. They don't panic under duress; their offense remains methodical, their defense tightens, and they trust their system to generate the right shot. This isn't luck—it's a repeatable skill.
For the Grizzlies, their 2-4 record reveals a pattern of late-game fractures. The mounting stress manifests as costly turnovers, rushed possessions, and defensive lapses. Their challenge isn't a lack of ability, but the absence of a trusted process to fall back on when the pressure is highest. Tonight's game is a referendum on their resolve against a team that weaponizes that very pressure.
