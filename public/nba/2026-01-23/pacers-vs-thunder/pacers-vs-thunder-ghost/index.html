Pressure Points
- The emotional state of the Pacers in the opening minutes. Do they play with defiant anger or look defeated from the tip?
- Pascal Siakam's ability to be a solo offensive engine against the Thunder's elite defense.
- Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's approach. Does he look to put the game away early or let it come to him?
- The first major Thunder run. Indiana's response will reveal if Rick Carlisle's message about competing is taking hold.
- How long OKC's starters play. An early exit would signal total dominance; extended minutes could mean Indiana is hanging around.
What This Means
This game is a raw look at the brutal consequences of championship-level basketball. It's not about who wins, but about how they play. For the Pacers, it's a test of character, a chance to show professional pride on the ground where their ambitions were crushed. For the Thunder, it's a measure of their championship DNA—whether they can remain disciplined and focused against a broken opponent, proving they have the mindset to sustain a dynasty, not just win a single title.
