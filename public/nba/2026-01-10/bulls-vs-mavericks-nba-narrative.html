The New Order: Dallas Mavericks vs. Chicago Bulls (January 10, 2026)
Tonight at the United Center, the NBA landscape feels significantly different than it did just twelve months ago. Gone are the days of the Luka Dončić versus Zach LaVine showdowns. In their place stands a matchup of two franchises in the throes of radical transformation. The Chicago Bulls (-3) host the Dallas Mavericks (+121) in a contest defined by who isn't on the floor as much as who is.
#### The Betting Landscape
The market has settled on the Bulls as a 3-point home favorite, a line that seems largely influenced by the Mavericks' disastrous 4-13 road record. Despite the Bulls' own four-game losing streak, the United Center remains a stabilizing force. The Over/Under of 233.5 reflects Chicago's high-octane, low-defense style (ranking 26th in points allowed), but the total has seen some downward pressure due to the confirmed absence of Chicago's new primary engine, Josh Giddey (hamstring).
#### The Rise of Cooper Flagg
For Dallas, the narrative begins and ends with the 2025 #1 overall pick, Cooper Flagg. Following the seismic trade that sent Luka Dončić to the Lakers, Flagg has been handed the keys to the franchise. Averaging 19.1 points and 6.6 rebounds, Flagg is the current frontrunner for Rookie of the Year. Tonight, he faces a Bulls perimeter defense that has been leaky all season. If Anthony Davis is cleared to play through his hand injury, Dallas will have a formidable inside-out duo that could challenge the Bulls' status as favorites.
#### Chicago's Guard Crisis
Billy Donovan is facing a personnel nightmare. With Giddey out and Coby White managing a nagging calf injury, the Bulls are forced to rely on Tre Jones and Ayo Dosunmu to maintain their 3rd-ranked pace. The Bulls' offensive efficiency is expected to take a hit without Giddey's near-triple-double production. This absence is the primary reason the moneyline has narrowed, as public perception wavers on Chicago’s ability to create half-court offense.
#### The Storyline
This is more than a mid-season game; it is a preview of the next era. Dallas is testing whether their post-Luka rebuild can find traction behind Flagg, while Chicago is trying to prove they can survive an injury-depleted stretch to remain in the Eastern Conference play-in hunt. Expect a high-variance affair where the star power of Flagg and the veteran presence of Nikola Vucevic dictate whether the spread covers or the total plummets.
> HEATCHECKS EDGE
The Cooper Flagg era isn't coming—it's already here. While the world stares at the 'post-Luka vacuum' in Dallas, they are missing the emergence of a superstar who is about to feast on a Chicago defense that couldn't stop a nosebleed. The Bulls are reeling from four straight losses and the absence of Josh Giddey leaves them rudderless in the half-court. Chicago plays fast (3rd in pace), but they play bad, and Flagg is the exact type of physical anomaly that punishes their 26th-ranked scoring defense. I’m fading the Bulls’ home-court 'edge' and backing the phenom. Take the Mavericks (+3) and sprinkle the Moneyline (+121); Flagg is the best player on the court tonight, and in the NBA, that's where the money goes. Bet the Over 233.5 as well—this will be a track meet that Dallas wins.
