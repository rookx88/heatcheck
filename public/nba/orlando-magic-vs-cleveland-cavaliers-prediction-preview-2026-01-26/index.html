The core of this matchup lies in that immediate history. The pressure on the Magic is immense—to prove the previous blowout was an anomaly and not the new standard of this head-to-head. Cleveland faces a different kind of pressure: the expectation to win and the challenge of dispatching a desperate opponent who has nothing left to lose. The game will likely be decided by which team better handles its unique situational stress.
