Brewers vs. Dodgers: Skubal's Arrival Hasn't Fixed Anything

Everyone lost their minds when the Dodgers landed Tarik Skubal. Another star, another payroll flex, another round of the same argument: this team is breaking the sport. Small market fans posted the receipts. Analysts asked if MLB needs a salary cap. And then the games actually started, and Los Angeles has barely been able to buy a win since he arrived.

That is the tension sitting over this series. The Dodgers have the名 arm, the depth, the resume, everything that is supposed to make a team unbeatable on paper. The Brewers show up as the team that keeps punching holes in that theory, night after night, without anyone quite explaining why.

Milwaukee gets another shot at it. The storyline says the Dodgers should be steamrolling the league right now. The actual results say something else entirely. Which version shows up when the Brewers and Dodgers get on the field tonight is the whole question on the board.

  • Every team in baseball watched the Dodgers add Skubal and braced for a bulldozer. Instead LA has barely won since he showed up.
  • The gap between the roster and the results is becoming its own story. The Brewers have been on the right side of that gap lately.
  • Tonight either the superteam narrative starts catching up to the name-brand roster, or Milwaukee keeps making a mess of the theory.
