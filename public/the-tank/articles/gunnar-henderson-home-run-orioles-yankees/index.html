Wed, Aug 19 · 6:35 PM ET

Gunnar Henderson HR Prop: Orioles vs Yankees Preview

Aaron Boone said it out loud this week: Aaron Judge is starting his hitting progression, the first real step back from a fractured rib. That is good news for the Yankees and bad news for anyone hoping this series stays low stakes. Judge is not in this lineup yet. Gunnar Henderson is, and he is the one currently making a Yankees pitching staff look every bit as unsettled as its record suggests.

This is the last look at this version of the Orioles-Yankees rivalry before Judge's return changes the equation entirely. Henderson has been the engine of Baltimore's lineup all year, the guy pitching staffs game plan around, and right now he gets to do it against a New York staff that has not figured out how to game plan around him. Every homer he hits this week lands a little differently, knowing the guy who used to anchor that Yankees lineup is watching from the dugout instead of standing in the box.

So the question is simple. Does Henderson leave the yard tonight, one more time, before this series stops being about what New York is missing?

  • Aaron Boone confirmed it this week: Judge is starting his hitting progression. That means he's close, but this series is still happening without him.
  • Gunnar Henderson doesn't care about timelines. He's been the problem for opposing pitching staffs all year, and right now that includes a Yankees staff still looking for its footing.
  • This is the last Orioles-Yankees series before Judge's return reshapes everything. Henderson gets one more night as the biggest bat on the field.
  • Over 0.5 home runs, or does the Yankees staff finally figure him out first?
