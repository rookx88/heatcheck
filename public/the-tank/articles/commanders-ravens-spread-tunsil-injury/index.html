Commanders vs. Ravens Spread: Life Without Tunsil

Laremy Tunsil is done for the season, and Jayden Daniels just lost the guy whose entire job was keeping him upright. A torn triceps ends Tunsil's year and hands Washington's line to whoever's next on the depth chart, right as the Commanders walk into Baltimore.

This is the part where the tape gets ugly or the Commanders prove the operation runs deeper than one left tackle. Daniels has escaped pressure with his legs before, but a compromised edge against a Ravens front looking to get after the quarterback is a different kind of pressure test than a bad series here and there. Baltimore knows exactly what just happened in Washington's building, and defensive coordinators do not forget injury news like this.

So the spread becomes the whole story. Is Washington's roster deep enough to hide one massive hole, or does Daniels spend Sunday running for his life? That's the number on the board, and that's the question this game actually answers.

  • Laremy Tunsil tears his triceps and Washington's line goes from settled to scrambling overnight.
  • Daniels has bailed out bad protection with his legs before, but Baltimore's front is built to punish exactly this kind of hole.
  • The Commanders either show they've got answers on the roster, or this spread turns into a long afternoon fast.
  • Cover or collapse, this line is the first real read on what Washington looks like without its blindside guy.
