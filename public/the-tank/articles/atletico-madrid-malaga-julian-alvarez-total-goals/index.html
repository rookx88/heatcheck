Atletico Madrid vs Malaga: Over/Under 2.5 Without Alvarez

Enrique Cerezo keeps saying the same thing. Julian Alvarez is not for sale, not to Barcelona, not to anyone. Then Atletico Madrid leave him out of the squad to face Malaga and suddenly nobody believes the president at all. The Independent called it a drop. Simeone has not called it anything. And now Arsenal and Barcelona are both refreshing their phones a little more often than usual.

Whatever this is, a rest, a message, a genuine crack in the relationship, it lands on a Wednesday night where Atletico still have to score goals without their best one. Malaga are not here to make that easy. A front line missing its main threat against an away side happy to sit in and frustrate is exactly the kind of matchup that turns into a grind instead of a shootout.

So forget the transfer noise for ninety minutes. The question in front of you is simpler and just as live. Do Atletico and Malaga combine for more than two and a half goals, or does this Alvarez subplot bleed into a night where the goals just do not show up.

  • Cerezo says Alvarez isn't going anywhere. Then Atletico bench him completely for Malaga and the exit rumors get louder, not quieter.
  • The Independent is calling it a drop. Simeone isn't confirming or denying anything.
  • Arsenal and Barcelona are both watching closely. So is anyone who bet on Atletico goals tonight.
  • Malaga away, no Alvarez, plenty of noise. Does this game actually produce goals or does the drama stay off the pitch.
