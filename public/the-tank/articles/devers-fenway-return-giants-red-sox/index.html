Rafael Devers Returns to Fenway: Giants vs Red Sox HR Watch

Rafael Devers is walking back into Fenway Park wearing a Giants uniform, and nobody in Boston has made peace with that sentence yet. This isn't just a road trip stop on the schedule. It's the guy who was supposed to be a Red Sox lifer, standing in the same batter's box, against the team that decided he wasn't part of the plan anymore.

Every at bat this weekend gets read as a referendum. A hit is a statement. A strikeout gets turned into a headline either way. Somewhere in that noise sits a simple prop: does Devers hit a home run in this game. No storyline decides that for you. The pitching matchup does, the swing does, the moment does or doesn't get to him.

But this is the one game all season where a Devers home run wouldn't just be a stat. It would be the loudest possible answer to a crowd that hasn't decided yet whether to boo him or thank him. Does he give Fenway that moment, or does the night stay quiet?
