A Test of Resolve
Espanyol, a top-five side, is suddenly vulnerable. A four-game winless streak, compounded by a major injury to attacker Javi Puado, has them reeling. Alaves is not a powerhouse, but their recent win gives them a puncher's chance against a team questioning itself. This match is less about tactics and more about nerve.

What This Means
This match is a measure of character. For Espanyol, it's a test of whether they have the mettle of a true European contender, capable of arresting a slide before it becomes a full-blown crisis. For Alaves, it represents an opportunity to prove their recent win was not an anomaly but the start of a resilient turn, demonstrating they can compete with and unsettle the league's upper tier on the road. The result will reveal much about the underlying resolve of both clubs.
