Purdy’s Return to the Linc: Redemption or Repeat?

// Brock Purdy returns to Philadelphia for a Wild Card showdown 3 years after his injury. Can the 49ers overcome a decimated pass rush against the defending Super Bowl champions?

> MATCHUP: NFL | Philadelphia Eagles vs San Francisco 49ers | DATE: 2026-01-11
← BACK
> IMAGE_ASSET [LOADED]
The Linc Never Forgets
When Brock Purdy steps onto the turf at Lincoln Financial Field this Sunday, he won’t just be looking at the Eagles’ secondary; he’ll be staring down the ghosts of January 2023. It has been three years since the 'Mr. Irrelevant' narrative was nearly ended by a torn UCL in this very building. Today, Purdy is anything but irrelevant—he is the $265 million face of the 49ers franchise, but the pressure has never been higher.
The Defending Champ’s Hubris

Nick Sirianni and the Philadelphia Eagles are operating with the quiet confidence of a team that reached the mountaintop just eleven months ago. By resting Jalen Hurts and Saquon Barkley in Week 18, Sirianni signaled to the league that he isn't afraid of losing momentum. He has a Super Bowl LIX ring to back up the arrogance. Meanwhile, Kyle Shanahan is reeling. The 49ers were 60 minutes away from a first-round bye and home-field advantage before a stunning Week 18 collapse against Seattle forced them into 'the hard way.'
The Missing Tooth in the Niners' Defense

The most critical subplot of this matchup isn't Purdy's arm—it's the 49ers' nonexistent pass rush. In 2024 and 2025, the San Francisco defense was built on the back of Nick Bosa. With Bosa sidelined, the Niners finished dead last in the NFL with a mere 20 sacks. Jalen Hurts, protected by a returning Lane Johnson, will have an eternity in the pocket to pick apart a secondary that has looked increasingly fragile under pressure.
Saquon vs. CMC: The Clash of Eras

This is a rare postseason meeting of two generational talents at running back. Saquon Barkley proved he was the missing piece for Philly's championship puzzle last year, and despite a slight dip in 2025, he remains the most explosive weapon in the NFC. On the other side, Christian McCaffrey is playing through back stiffness to keep a fading 49ers window open. If San Francisco can't get to Hurts, McCaffrey will have to play the game of his life just to keep pace with an Eagles offense that is rested, healthy, and playing in front of a bloodthirsty home crowd.
The emotional disparity in this game is a chasm. The Eagles are the defending champions, rested, and playing at home with a fully healthy Jalen Hurts. The 49ers are mentally exhausted after blowing the division in the final week and are statistically the worst pass-rushing team in the league without Bosa. Brock Purdy’s return to the site of his greatest trauma while carrying the weight of a $265M contract against a team that doesn't respect him (see: Deebo Samuel's quotes) is a recipe for a meltdown. **Eagles -5.5 is the play; Philly's 'Billion Dollar Front Line' will give Hurts all day to dismantle a Niners team that is doing it 'the hard way' because they're simply not the same team they were three years ago.**
