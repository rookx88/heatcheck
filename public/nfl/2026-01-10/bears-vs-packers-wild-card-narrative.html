The Ghost of Ownership: A New Era Meets An Old Haunt
For three decades, the rivalry between the Chicago Bears and the Green Bay Packers has felt less like a contest and more like an inheritance. From Brett Favre to Aaron Rodgers, the keys to the NFC North were kept firmly in a safe in Green Bay, with Rodgers famously reminding the Soldier Field faithful, "I still own you."
But as the 2026 Wild Card weekend arrives, the air in Wisconsin carries a different scent. It’s not just the sub-zero wind chill—it’s the arrival of Caleb Williams.
The Market's Respect for 'The Frozen Throne'

Oddsmakers have set the Green Bay Packers as -3.5 point favorites, a number that sits in the uncomfortable 'dead zone' for bettors. It suggests that while the talent gap between these two rosters has evaporated, the 'Lambeau Tax' remains. The Packers are -175 on the Moneyline, implying a 63.6% probability of victory. This isn't just a reflection of Jordan Love’s stellar 2025 campaign; it’s a nod to the institutional memory of a franchise that simply does not lose these games at home.
The Caleb Factor

Caleb Williams enters this matchup with a unique shield: he has no memory of the 90s or 2000s heartbreak. In a December push that saw him throw 10 touchdowns and only one interception, Williams has played with a clinical defiance. When asked about the "Ownership" narrative, his response was curt: "That ends this weekend."
This psychological shift is what makes the Bears a live underdog at +150. For the first time in years, the Bears' offense isn't built to 'survive' the Packers; it's built to outpace them.

The Over/Under has seen a significant drop, settling at 44.5. Early week projections had this in the high 40s, but the forecast of 15-degree temperatures has the betting public eyeing the Under. The Strategic Pivot
