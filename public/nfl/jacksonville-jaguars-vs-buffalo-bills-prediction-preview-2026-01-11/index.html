The Jacksonville Jaguars travel to face the Buffalo Bills in the AFC Wild Card round on Sunday, January 11, 2026, in a matchup that pits surging momentum against established pressure. At stake is a spot in the Divisional Round and a chance to validate a season's worth of adjustments. For Jacksonville, the story is one of transformation. The offensive evolution, which saw quarterback Trevor Lawrence reclaim command of the system, has generated a powerful wave of late-season momentum. They arrive in Buffalo not as an underdog, but as a team that believes it has forged a sustainable identity. The critical question, however, is how that newfound confidence holds up under the unique stress of playoff football, particularly in one of the league's most hostile road environments.
